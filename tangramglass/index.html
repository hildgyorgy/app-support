TangramGlass

A Liquid Glass playground

About

TangramGlass is a calm desktop playground inspired by the traditional tangram puzzle and Apple's Liquid Glass visual language. Move, rotate and combine translucent shapes to create playful compositions.

System requirements

TangramGlass is designed for Apple Silicon Macs and requires macOS 26.5 or later.

Get TangramGlass on the App Store

How to use

  • Drag a piece to move it.
  • Scroll over a piece to rotate it; use the trackpad for very smooth rotation.
  • Double-click a piece to rotate it by 45°.
  • Option-double-click the parallelogram to flip it.
  • Use the Share button to export your composition as an artwork image.

Features

  • One, two or three tangram sets.
  • Customizable corner radius.
  • Liquid and frosted glass appearance.
  • Natural, tinted and rainbow color modes.
  • Artwork export and sharing.

Privacy Policy

TangramGlass does not collect, store or share personal information.

All settings are stored locally on your device using system storage.

TangramGlass does not require a user account and does not transmit personal data to external servers.

Artwork export uses the macOS Screen Recording permission only to capture the TangramGlass window. The captured image remains on your device unless you explicitly choose to share it using the macOS sharing panel.
